07 · faq

%man sequel

is this financial advice?

No. A "greenlit" verdict only means Part I topped, faded and still has holders. Sequel never says buy, and it never will.

is a sequel a cto?

No. A CTO takes over the old coin and its old chart, with every top buyer still holding above you. A sequel is a new coin that says openly it is Part II. It starts at zero and borrows the story, not the bags.

where does the data come from?

DexScreener for the market, GeckoTerminal for holders and the daily chart, pump.fun for the coin's own peak. All public, read live, no keys. Peak means the highest market cap those sources report.

does it launch the coin for me?

Not yet. Today the studio builds the kit and opens Raydium LaunchLab with everything copied, and you launch it yourself. One-click launch is next, paid for by $SEQUEL's fees.

can I make a sequel of any coin?

You can read any coin. What you launch is your call. Every kit says it is Part II of the original and never claims to be the original team. Don't use art you don't have the rights to.

will sequel DM me?

Never. No presale, no airdrop form, no support account. Anyone who DMs you as sequel is not us.
